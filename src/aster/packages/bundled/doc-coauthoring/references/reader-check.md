# Reader check

1. Can the intended reader state the document's purpose after the first paragraph?
2. Are the necessary background and abbreviations introduced before use?
3. Does each major claim have a real source, and does the source actually support it?
4. Are measured facts distinguished from estimates, recommendations and assumptions?
5. Can the reader tell what decision is required, by whom and by when?
6. Are alternatives and material limitations acknowledged without hiding the recommendation?
7. Do headings, tables and examples agree with the prose and the latest accepted revision?

Record each issue with its section, evidence and proposed repair. Do not fabricate reader feedback. This workflow does not submit, publish or send the document.
